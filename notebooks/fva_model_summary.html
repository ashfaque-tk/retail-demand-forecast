Model Selection Checklist

  • Compare mean FVA across all windows.
  • Check median FVA to avoid conclusions driven by a few windows.
  • Check win rate for consistency across windows.
  • Compare MAE and WRMSSE separately.
  • Check whether accuracy improvements translate into lower inventory cost.
  • Inspect the window-by-window results before selecting the final deployment model.
